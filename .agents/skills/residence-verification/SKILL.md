name: residence-verification
description: Independently verify the observatory's source numbers, derived statistics, legal wording and public claims before an evidence release. Use for release review or a disputed fact.
---

Read `AGENTS.md`. Be a separate reviewer from the author of the claims. Do not edit the observations you audit; report a finding and let the author fix it.

Independently inspect the primary document, not only another researcher's extraction. Reproduce critical values and formulas. Check population, dates, metric definition, units, stock vs flow, missing values and denominators. For copies of official letters record that authenticity is not independently established on an agency website.

Check page text, titles, citation snippets and social previews: caveats must survive sharing. A mean of concluded cases is not an individual wait forecast; a court merit rate is not a probability of success; a scenario is not a prediction. Verify legal statements against current law and judgments, preserving conditions and scope.

Review the public file inventory for private records, credentials and copyrighted full text. Do not claim legal certification or absolute truth. Write `verification/` results with status, date, reviewed version/claim IDs, reproducible evidence, blocking findings and limitations. Re-review corrections. Approval covers only the explicit snapshot and scope.
