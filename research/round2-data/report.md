Audit date:2026-10-20. Scope: frozen release1.0.0,1780 observations; no edits to `data/curated`. This reviewer wrote the original production pipeline. ## Phase1 — reproduce the core

`scripts/round2_data_audit.py` independently re-read the cached Sejm HTML, official NSA XLS, direct UdSC portal JSON, and Glos-hosted official PDF/XLSX copies. All1780 published values were reproduced, with zero numerical mismatches.32 source/supporting snapshots were rehashed against their actual bytes. Court calculations were rebuilt from hearing/non-public counts, and all16 regional counts reconciled with the national row and stock-flow identities. The separate QA file records exact scope and methods.

For UdSC,192 numeric cells were re-extracted by PDF coordinates. Two bad OCR cells were visually reread as472 and145 on a freshly rendered page3. The old verified transcription was not an input. The **ORGAN column is redacted**, so geographic identity is not independently legible. Row assignments remain reconstructed using the published order and corroborating2023 figures. Numeric reproduction does not remove that limitation or authenticate an original electronic signature. The national sum of the16 rows is independent of which region label is assigned to each row.

Important findings and limits:

1. The shared `concluded_duration` definition is too broad when it states submission-to-resolution for every MSWiA series. UdSC explicitly defines those endpoints; the native18089 reply does not establish every system start-event. Proposed wording: reported administrative duration; submission-to-decision explicitly confirmed where the source says so. Core remains unchanged for editor/versioned correction.
2. Means concern a resolved cohort, not everyone waiting; composition/backlog clearance can increase a mean without slower work on comparable cases. Source-wide causal claims about staffing, legislation or MOS are not identified by these aggregates.
3. Warehouse2026 has no established operational cut-off. Its48 count/mean/max rows must remain period-ambiguous and outside current annual baselines. Report-generation date09.09.2026 is not a data cut-off.
4.803223 UdSC open cases on01.07.2026 and463487 art.114 cases in the MSWiA2025 reply have different populations and dates. Portal2025 decisions445462 and warehouse all-outcome resolutions398337 also have different scopes. Neither difference demonstrates an error or a common trend.
5. NSA6272 is broader than residence permits. Annual upheld/(upheld+dismissed) is a disposition share, not a filing-cohort success probability. There is no case-level bridge from administrative waiting to court outcomes. Opening court stocks are01.01; closing stocks31.12.
6. Reproducing an upstream number checks extraction, not completeness, truth of the administrative database, causal interpretation, or representative experience of all applicants. No national mean can be obtained by averaging regional means without valid weights.

The official portal case-type mapping is now directly evidenced by its REST dictionary: `caseType1=Pobyt czasowy`, `caseType3=Pobyt rezydenta dlugoterminowego UE`. The saved evidence adds a previously missing primary definition check; it does not alter old numeric values. [Official dictionary](https://migracje.gov.pl/wp-json/udscmap/v1/caseType).

## New official context, kept separate

`eurostat-context.json` is a new direct official API extraction outside the frozen core:15 observations for2021–2025, Poland and EU27 totals plus Belarus citizenship in Poland. Returned codes/labels were checked: `geo=PL/EU27_2020`, `citizen=TOTAL/BY`, `reason=TOTAL`, `duration=TOTAL`, `unit=PER`, `freq=A`. Status is pending independent review. It is **context-not-waiting**.

TOTAL means the citizenship total within Eurostat's non-EU residence-permit collection, not all migrants. The collection concerns authorisations of at least3months; a first permit can follow a qualifying gap after an old permit. Long-stay visas or equivalent national statuses can be included. It is neither a unique-new-arrival count nor the same scope as Polish voivode temporary-residence resolutions. EU27 already includes Poland. No flag is saved as null, not a claim that breaks cannot exist. [Collection information](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/migration-asylum/managed-migration/information-data), [metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/migr_resval_esms.htm).

## Phase3 — low-cost data and survey design

Keep GitHub Pages static. A small Cloudflare Worker handles only survey submission/edit/delete, server-side Turnstile validation, schema/range checks and prepared D1 writes. Origin allowlisting is useful browser control, not authentication; Turnstile hostname/action/single-use checks are required. No PDF parsing or LLM calls belong on this submission path. R2 is optional private content-addressed storage for source snapshots and restricted exports, not a public survey dump. Staff review/exports require separate authorization.

As checked in official documentation on20.10.2026, Workers Free allows100000 requests/day and10ms CPU per invocation; a Paid account starts at$5/month. D1 Free includes5million row reads/day,100000 row writes/day and5GB total storage. R2 Standard includes10GB-month storage and operation allowances; operations can still incur charges. Turnstile's Free plan supports unlimited challenges. These are allowances, not a guaranteed project bill; index maintenance, abuse, domain/email, retention and overages matter. Start with a low-volume indexed D1 design and static aggregate reads; benchmark before promising that it fits Free. [Workers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), [D1](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/), [R2](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/), [Turnstile](https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/plans/).

Suggested incremental pipeline: weekly conditional GETs using ETag/Last-Modified when available; hash bytes; content-address changed snapshots; normalize only changed eligible sources; create a candidate diff; run dimension/date/unit/cohort/source and reconciliation tests; obtain a separate reviewer approval; build a versioned immutable release and small manifest. A stable latest pointer changes only after approval. Revisions must show old/new values and affected IDs. Eurostat provides current versions rather than a full revision archive, so local versioned snapshots are necessary. Keep Python extraction on scheduled CI/local jobs; use human review and selective OCR for changed scans. Do not spend LLM tokens reprocessing unchanged documents. [Eurostat API/versioning](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/user-guides/data-browser/api-data-access/api-introduction).

Suggested retention is a **design proposal**, not an implemented/legal policy: unsubmitted browser data only in-session; raw IP never stored by application; short-lived rotating abuse HMACs up to24h; failed payload bodies not logged; unverified optional email purged after7days; opted-in contact after90days unless renewed; private questionnaire records up to24months with delete/edit token; released nonidentifying aggregates and official-source hashes retained with release history. Provider logs/backups and GDPR controller/basis/notice must be configured and checked before accepting real responses. Contact email is separate optional consent and a separate restricted store, never part of the public analytical dataset.

Survey: ask office, permit type/legal-basis group, submission/fingerprints/decision/card stages, dates with precision, current endpoint/outcome and observation date. Separate completed submission→decision from ongoing right-censored cases and decision→plastic. Month-only dates imply intervals; do not invent exact midmonth dates. Track updates using a random case token and hashed edit secret. Turnstile and idempotency keys limit abuse but cannot prove one respondent/person. Office+month+permit similarity is a soft duplicate flag, not grounds for collapsing different people. Do not collect PESEL, case signatures, passports, documents or unrestricted case narratives.

Publish only coarse aggregates with a proposed small-cell threshold of10 and complementary suppression; that is a privacy rule, not statistical adequacy. Report recruitment channels, response counts, exclusions, date precision, missingness, completed/ongoing mix and attrition. Cross-sectional ongoing cases can be length-biased; even Kaplan–Meier requires assumptions about sampling, delayed entry and censoring. It cannot make this self-selected survey nationally representative. National administrative context comes from official sources; chats and survey describe participating samples. Do not rank offices' real performance from these volunteer responses.

X can supply public leads via authorized APIs or manually submitted links, not a representative sentiment/waiting sample. Official docs currently describe credit/pay-per-use, recent7-day search and paid full-archive access. Configure spending caps, store permitted identifiers/links and observe deletion/compliance requirements; do not scrape, bypass access restrictions, or promise full coverage. No X collection was run. [Pricing](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing), [search scope](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/search/introduction).

`source-roadmap.json` gives bounded official-source work packages; `offices.json` provides16 verified directory entries with explicit fallback statuses. National completeness is a property to document per administrative collection, not something conferred merely by a government hostname. None of these research outputs authorizes unattended publication.
