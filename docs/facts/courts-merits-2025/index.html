29.5% upheld — among merits rulings only

WSA, 2025, inactivity and delay, category 6272: 432 upheld and 1,032 dismissed complaints. 432 / (432 + 1,032) = 29.5%. The category includes visas, residence, expulsion and other foreigner cases.

The denominator excludes, among other outcomes, 1,873 procedural rejections. This is neither a share of new filings nor an individual’s probability of success.
